# Mama B's Kitchen

THE LOVABLE PROMPT Build a modern, responsive landing page for Mama B’s Kitchen, a home catering business based in Abuja, Nigeria.

Business Description

Mama B’s Kitchen provides delicious traditional Nigerian food for events, office lunches, and weekend family orders. The business currently receives customers mainly through WhatsApp and word of mouth. The goal of this website is to give customers an easy way to view the menu, place advance food orders, and contact the business for event catering quotes.

Design Direction

Create a warm, elegant, welcoming Nigerian food brand.

Use:

Primary colour: Burgundy

Secondary colour: Cream

Clean white/light backgrounds where appropriate

High-quality food photography

Modern, professional typography

Warm and appetizing visual presentation

Plenty of spacing and clear sections

Rounded buttons and cards

Subtle animations and hover effects

Fully responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop

The design should feel trustworthy and professional while still maintaining the warmth and homely character of a Nigerian catering business.
